# Sources

- Jason Long and Adam Zsolt Wagner, *The largest projective cube-free subsets of Z_{2^n}*, 2018. [arXiv:1810.01225](https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.01225). Conjecture 5.1 states the `5/8` bound, and the paper reports a Gurobi check for `n <= 7`. The journal version is Europ. J. Combin. 2019, doi 10.1016/j.ejc.2019.05.005.
- Yuchen Meng, *On Cube-Free Problems*, Electronic Journal of Combinatorics 33(1) (2026), P1.16. [combinatorics.org](https://www.combinatorics.org/ojs/index.php/eljc/article/view/v33i1p16). It gives the unconditional `2/3` bound. It's also arXiv:2311.12318 (2023, revised 2025), where the bound is Theorem 3.1.

`ledger.ndjson` records the sha256 of our plain-text extraction of Meng's paper. We left extractions and PDFs out because the papers belong to their authors, and both links serve the full text.
