# Sources

- Jason Long and Adam Zsolt Wagner, *The largest projective cube-free subsets of
  Z_{2^n}*, 2018. [arXiv:1810.01225](https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.01225). Conjecture 5.1
  states the `5/8` bound. The paper reports a Gurobi check for `n <= 7`.
- Yuchen Meng, *On Cube-Free Problems*, Electronic Journal of Combinatorics 33(1)
  (2026), P1.16.
  [combinatorics.org](https://www.combinatorics.org/ojs/index.php/eljc/article/view/v33i1p16).
  It gives the unconditional `2/3` bound.

`ledger.ndjson` records the sha256 of the plain-text extraction of each paper that
was read. The extractions and PDFs are left out because the papers belong to their
authors. Both links above serve the full text.
